Target scenarios for 2026-27

🥇 To Become National #1 (Beat Chandigarh 766.0)

Need +42.2 points. Chandigarh's edge sits almost entirely in Infrastructure (150.1 vs 136.9) and Governance (106.0 vs 102.9). Closing both gaps alone yields +16.3; the remainder must come from Learning Outcomes.

⬆ To Reach UTTAM-3 (Score ≥ 761)

Need +37.2 points. Would place DNH&DD in the top grade band currently occupied only by Chandigarh.

🛡️ To Defend Rank #2

Punjab is only 0.2 points behind and Kerala 7.0 behind. Any slip in Access or Governance could cost two ranks. This is the tightest competitive band in the country.

⚠️ The Learning Outcomes Cliff

LO is frozen at 100.0 because PRS 2024 data was carried forward. When the next PARAKH survey lands, this score will move for real. At 41.7% of optimal, LO is by far the weakest domain and represents 140 of the 276.2 remaining points.

🌟 To Achieve UTKARSH (Score ≥ 941)

Need +217.2 points. Down from +257.8 last year. Multi-year aspirational goal, achievable only through a Learning Outcomes transformation.

📋 STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS FOR 2026-27

🧭 Priority Action Plan
Target: UTTAM-3 grade and National Rank #1
  • 🎯 Priority #1 — Learning Outcomes, now urgent: LO has been frozen at 100.0 for two cycles on PRS 2024 data. When the next PARAKH survey runs, the real position gets exposed. At 41.7% of optimal it holds 140 of the 276.2 remaining points. Build the FLN and Grade 6/9 remediation pipeline now, not after the survey is announced.
  • 🛡️ Priority #2 — Defend Rank #2: Punjab trails by 0.2 points and Kerala by 7.0. Treat every domain as non-negotiable. Set an internal floor of 723.8 and target 761+ for UTTAM-3.
  • 📉 Priority #3 — Reverse the Access decline: Access fell 66.1 → 64.6 and slipped from UTTAM-1 to UTTAM-2. Kerala (73.8) and Puducherry (73.8) hold UTKARSH. Focus on NER at Higher Secondary and retention at secondary level.
  • 🏛️ Consolidate the Governance win: GP jumped +26.3 points to 102.9, now #2 in India. Lock in digital attendance, fund release timelines and Vidyanjali gains so this does not regress the way it did in 2023-24.
  • 🏗️ Push Infrastructure to UTTAM-1: At 136.85 DNH&DD holds UTTAM-2, one of only four states. Chandigarh is at 150.1. Closing that 13-point gap needs solar panels, rainwater harvesting and vocational placement indicators.
  • 👩‍🏫 Teacher Education recovered — hold it: TE&T rebounded 79.4 → 84.8 (UTTAM-1). Kerala (92.4) and Lakshadweep (96.2) show the UTKARSH ceiling is reachable.
  • 📊 PGI War-Room, indicator level: With the top four states inside 11 points of each other, aggregate tracking is no longer enough. Assign owners to individual indicators, especially the 15 Governance and 15 Infrastructure sub-indicators.
  • 🔗 Benchmark against Chandigarh specifically: It is the only state above DNH&DD. Its lead is concentrated in Infrastructure (+13.3), Governance (+3.1) and Learning Outcomes (+10.6). A focused study visit on those three domains is the highest-value learning exercise available.
